01 / The problem

Generic marketplaces were too broad for campus life.

Students needed a faster and safer way to sell everyday essentials, but large marketplaces were not designed around the close community of a college campus. Distance, uncertainty, and anonymous profiles made simple transactions feel unnecessarily risky.

02 / The solution

A marketplace where proximity creates confidence.

DormDeal gives students a focused place to list and sell their items within the campus community. Real name profiles create accountability, while a real time feed makes newly listed items visible without friction. The result is a more direct path from listing an item to finding an interested student.

03 / Engineering

  • 01Real time data architecture for fast, continuously updated marketplace feeds.
  • 02Strict access controls that protect user data and preserve transaction integrity.
  • 03Accountable profile structures built around real names instead of anonymous handles.
  • 04Optimized media delivery that keeps listing images useful without unnecessary bandwidth costs.

04 / Outcome

From backend foundations to a live campus demo.

DormDeal was successfully deployed and exhibited at the NITJ Open Day in May 2026. The project translated backend decisions around security, performance, and media handling into a practical marketplace experience for the campus community.
